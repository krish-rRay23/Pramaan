Maker-Checker & Dual-Sign-Off Flow PHASE 2 ARCHITECTURE

Design specification for legitimate destination/bank-detail modifications (e.g. TVS Credit updating an institutional collections VPA, or customer disbursement bank details genuinely changing).

1. Maker Initiation (Branch / Ops Officer)

Operations officer proposes an update_bank_details or update_collections_vpa request with supporting audit documentation (cancelled cheque, Penny Drop verification, or treasury mandate).

2. Checker Dual Sign-Off (Risk Officer)

Mandatory segregated dual authorization by Risk & Compliance Officer using hardware token (FIDO2/WebAuthn). This dual approval re-signs the TVS Master Authority registry.

3. 12-Hour Cooldown & Out-of-Band Notice

Mandatory cooling-off window before the new VPA is accepted by the Policy Gate. Out-of-band alerts are dispatched to customer registered SIM/email to ensure non-repudiation.

Current Enforced Security Rule for update_bank_details:
Standard recovery agents (e.g. AGT-7701) and third-party partner agencies have zero authorization to modify banking destinations at runtime. Any runtime attempt to substitute bank account or VPA triggers immediate BLOCK and logs a critical security anomaly.

FUTURE ROADMAP / VISION ITEM

Strategic roadmap positioning TVS Credit ahead of impending NBFC fraud intelligence mandates through interaction-layer attestation.

DoT Digital Intelligence Platform (DIP) & FRI
  • The Department of Telecommunications (DoT) operates the Digital Intelligence Platform (DIP) with 1,600+ organizations onboarded (banks, UPI providers, telecoms, law enforcement).
  • Feeds the Financial Fraud Risk Indicator (FRI), scoring mobile numbers by real-time fraud risk using bank-submitted intelligence plus Chakshu and NCRP reports.
  • RBI 30 June 2025 Advisory: RBI advised all scheduled commercial banks, small finance banks, payments banks, and co-operative banks to integrate FRI. NBFCs are NOT named in that advisory.
Pramaan's "Get Ahead" Regulatory Position
  • Feeding DIP with Blocked Receipts: Pramaan's cryptographic trust receipts for blocked interactions (unauthorized VPAs, rogue caller numbers) are structured so they can feed DIP in the exact same schema banks use today.
  • Proactive NBFC Leadership: TVS Credit adopting this ahead of any regulatory extension to NBFCs is a "get ahead of it" stance, rather than waiting for an RBI mandate to catch up.
  • Interaction Layer vs. Number Layer: While DoT's Mobile Number Revocation List (MNRL) propagates fraud-linked SIMs across banking, Pramaan provides instant revocation at the interaction intent layer.
